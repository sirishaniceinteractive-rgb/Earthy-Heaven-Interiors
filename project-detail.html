The Oak
Residence.

Year2024Area2,850 sq ftScopeFull renovationPhotographyStudio Haven

The concept

For a young family in a nineteenth-century Brooklyn brownstone, we created a home that feels grounded, tactile, and completely of its time. A warm oak backbone runs throughout the rooms, connecting the original architecture to a softer, more contemporary way of living.

The feeling
of arriving.

Muted mineral tones, honest materials, and a rhythm of old and new give the house a calm, layered character. We opened the kitchen to the garden, restored the original trim, and designed each joinery moment to quietly earn its place.

Materials · European oak · Tumbled limestone · Hand-finished plaster · Linen · Aged bronze
