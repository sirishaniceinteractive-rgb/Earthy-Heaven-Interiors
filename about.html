Our story

Earthy Haven began with a simple belief: the spaces around us should make the everyday feel more considered.

Founded in New York in 2012 by Celia Hart, the studio has grown into a close-knit team working across residential, hospitality, and commercial interiors. Our work is grounded in the warmth of natural materials, the calm of thoughtful proportion, and an enduring respect for how a space is actually used.

We design with restraint, but never without feeling. The result is work that lives comfortably today and gathers meaning over time.

“A room should not announce itself. It should reveal itself, slowly, through the way it makes you feel.”
